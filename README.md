# Future_DS_01
This project is about Business sales dashboard in ecommerce in the field of Data analytics. 

📊 **Project Overview**
This interactive Power BI dashboard visualizes e-commerce sales performance across countries, years, and product categories.
It provides valuable insights into revenue distribution, sales trends, and top-performing products, enabling data-driven business decisions for sales optimization.

🎯 **Objectives**

Analyze sales trends over time (monthly & yearly).
Identify top 10 best-selling products by sales revenue.
Visualize country-wise revenue contribution.
Provide dynamic filters for country, year, and product description.
Present an interactive, executive-level dashboard for business insights.

🧰 **Tools & Technologies
Purpose	Tool Used**:

Data Cleaning	Microsoft Excel
Data Visualization	Power BI
Data Source	E-commerce Sales Dataset (2009–2011)

🧾 **Dataset Description**

The dataset contains sales transactions from an e-commerce platform with the following key columns:
Country, Customer ID, Description, Invoice, InvoiceDate, Price, Quantity, Revenue and StockCode

📈 **Dashboard Features**
1. **Sales Trends Over Time**
Line chart showing total revenue month-wise (Jan–Dec) across multiple years (2009–2011).
Identifies peak sales periods and seasonal trends.

2. **Top 10 Products by Sales Revenue**
Horizontal bar chart ranking the highest-selling products.
Helps understand customer preferences and top contributors to revenue.

3. **Sum of Revenue by Country**
Pie chart highlighting each country’s share of total revenue.
The United Kingdom contributes the majority of sales (≈83%).

4. **Dynamic Filters**
Country, Year, and Description slicers for customized insights.
Users can interactively explore specific product segments or time periods.

🔍 **Key Insights**

Top Country: United Kingdom contributes the largest share of total revenue (~82%).
Peak Sales: Highest sales recorded during November–December, indicating holiday shopping impact.
Top Product: Regency Cakestand 3 Tier generates the highest sales revenue.
Growth Trend: Positive sales growth towards year-end across all years.

🚀 **Workflow**

Imported raw e-commerce dataset into Power BI.
Performed data cleaning in Power Query (removed nulls, formatted dates, created revenue column).
Built measures for Sum of Revenue and Top N filtering.
Designed visuals (line, pie, and bar charts) and aligned filters for interactivity.
Enhanced report layout with consistent theme and readable color palette.

💡 **Business Impact**

This dashboard helps stakeholders:
Identify profitable countries and top-selling products.
Track monthly sales performance and detect trends.
Support strategic marketing and inventory planning based on data insights

📘 **Tools Used**

Power BI Desktop – Data modeling and visualization
Excel – Pre-cleaning raw data
DAX – Creating calculated columns and measures
